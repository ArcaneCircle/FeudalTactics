About

This is a web port of Feudal Tactics by Sesu8642 and contributors, a medieval strategy game inspired by Sean O'Connor's Slay. Game rules, map generation and enemy AI are ported from the original GPL-3.0-or-later Java source.

Original game: github.com/Sesu8642/FeudalTactics (GPL-3.0-or-later). Textures and rules come from that project; this web version is a fan port for webxdc messengers.

Runs fully offline inside a webxdc mini app. No tracking, no network access, no ads. Your games are saved locally in this chat's app storage.

Challenges: inside a messenger you can share a map to the chat and compare results with everyone who plays it.

How to Play

Each tile is colored to show which player owns it. Two or more connected tiles of the same color form a kingdom. Conquer the whole island to win: the game ends when one player owns 80% of all kingdom tiles.

Select a kingdom by tapping one of your tiles (white outline). Its finances are shown in the top left: Savings (+income). Each kingdom earns one coin per tile that has no tree on it. Units cost salary every turn.

Units & towers: buy a peasant for 10 coins, place it on one of your own tiles or on an enemy tile adjacent to your kingdom. Place a peasant on another unit to combine them into a stronger unit. Only knights and barons can destroy towers.

Unit Strength Salary
Peasant 1 2
Spearman 2 6
Knight 3 18
Baron 4 54

Protection: units, capitals and towers protect their tile and all adjacent tiles; a digit and a shield in the corner of a tile show its protection level. You need a stronger unit than the protection to conquer.

Capitals: if a capital is destroyed (or a tile is cut off), the kingdom's savings are lost and a new capital is placed. If a kingdom is split, only the part with the capital keeps the money.

Trees block income. Palm trees grow on coast and spread every round; oak trees spread when two are adjacent. Place a unit on a tree to chop it (the unit cannot act afterwards). Gravestones appear when units can't be paid and turn into trees.

Controls: tap a tile to select a kingdom, tap a unit to pick it up and tap a target to place/conquer/combine. Double-tap an empty tile of the selected kingdom to buy and place a peasant at once. Buy buttons are at the bottom (right-click = peasant, middle-click = tower, long press also buys a tower). Undo works within your turn and the game is saved automatically.

Challenges: in a messenger you can share the current map to the chat. Everyone who plays it gets the same island and posts their result, so you can compare who won in the fewest rounds.
